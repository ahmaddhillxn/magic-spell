Game Guide
Harley’s Plinko is a fun carnival-style game where you drop a ball onto a peg board and watch it bounce down to a prize multiplier. The goal is simple: land in a winning slot and multiply your bet for a thrilling payout.
1
The Game Board
The main play area is a triangular Plinko board filled with pegs, where a ball drops from the top and bounces randomly until it reaches the bottom.
2
Making Moves
  • The bet selector shows your current wager. Use the minus (–) and plus (+) buttons to adjust the bet amount.
  • The Auto Play button allows you to place multiple bets automatically without pressing the bet button each time.
  • The Speed button lets you make the ball reach its final position faster.
  • Once you are ready, press the BET button to drop the ball and start the round. The ball will bounce through the pegs and land in a multiplier slot.
3
Bet Selector
Choose your bet using the Bet Selector in the betting area, select a preset amount, then press Apply Bet to confirm your choice.
